## What to do if something breaks

1. **In the first 45 days:** Sweetwater return / exchange. Email Kyle (`KyleS@sweetwater.com` or via the support portal), reference invoice. They handle RMA.
2. **45 days – 1 year:** Manufacturer warranty. Sweetwater can still help route the RMA.
3. **After 1 year:** Out-of-warranty repair (Focusrite has a parts program; Akai is hit-or-miss post-discontinuation). Sweetwater's tech support line will quote.

If you find any of the above items cheaper at an authorized dealer (B&H, Sweetwater competitors, Guitar Center, etc.) within **90 days of purchase**, Sweetwater refunds the difference. Email Kyle with a screenshot/link.
